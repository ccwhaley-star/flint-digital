FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Straight answers. No jargon. If your question isn't here, call us.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. We handle everything — design, code, hosting, SEO, ads. You tell us about your business, we take it from there. You'll review and approve, but you'll never touch a line of code.

What if I already have a website?

We'll audit it for free and tell you honestly whether it makes sense to improve what you have or start fresh. Most template sites are worth replacing. Some just need SEO and content work. We'll give you the straight answer.

How fast can you actually launch?

Most websites go from kickoff to live in 5–10 business days. We move fast because our AI workflows eliminate the busywork that makes traditional agencies take 8–12 weeks. Your bottleneck is usually content and feedback, not us.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization. When someone asks ChatGPT "best plumber in Olathe" or searches Google and gets an AI Overview — AEO is what determines whether your business shows up in that answer. It's the next frontier of search, and most agencies aren't doing it yet.

What if it doesn't work?

Every retainer is month-to-month. If you're not seeing results, you cancel. No penalties, no guilt trips, no awkward breakup conversations. We keep your business by earning it.

Why are you so much cheaper than other agencies?

We use AI to do the work that other agencies pay teams of people to do — first drafts, research, competitive analysis, content outlines, ad copy variants. We're not cutting corners. We're cutting overhead.

What platform do you build on?

We hand-code every site in static HTML and deploy to Netlify. That means sub-second load times, perfect PageSpeed scores, zero hosting cost, and no WordPress plugins to update or hack. You own the code — it's yours forever.

Do you manage Google Ads?

Yes — and we build them in Expert Mode, not the Smart Mode that Google pushes on small businesses. That means proper conversion tracking, negative keywords, responsive search ads, and real optimization. Not just handing Google your credit card and hoping.

What does "reputation management" include?

We monitor your Google reviews, help you build a review generation strategy so happy customers actually leave reviews, and on our Dominate plan we respond to reviews on your behalf. A plumber with 87 five-star reviews beats a plumber with 7 every single time.

Can you help with e-commerce?

Yes. We build lightweight e-commerce solutions for small businesses — product pages, payment integration, and inventory basics. If you need a full Shopify-scale operation, we'll tell you that too. We don't oversell.
