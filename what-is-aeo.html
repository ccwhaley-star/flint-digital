What Is AEO and Why Your Small Business Needs It in 2026

← Insights  ·  Flint Digital  ·  4 min read

Ask ChatGPT for a plumber recommendation. Ask Perplexity which self-storage facility to use. Search Google and read the AI Overview at the top instead of clicking a single link. Millions of buying decisions now start — and end — inside an AI answer.

If your business isn't in that answer, you didn't lose the customer. You were never in the running.

AEO in one sentence

Answer Engine Optimization is the work of making your business the answer AI gives when someone asks a buying question. SEO earns you a spot in a list of links. AEO earns you a spot in the answer itself — and the answer is increasingly all people read.

How AI decides who to recommend

AI assistants don't guess. They read. Specifically, they favor businesses whose websites give them clean, verifiable material to work with:

  • Structured data — schema markup that states plainly what you do, where you operate, what you charge, and how to reach you.
  • Direct answers — pages that answer real questions in complete, quotable sentences instead of marketing haze.
  • A consistent identity — the same name, phone, and details everywhere the AI looks: your site, your Google Business Profile, your reviews.
  • Fast, clean pages — sites that machines can actually read. A bloated page builder buried in scripts is as hard for an AI to parse as it is slow for a human to load.

Why small businesses have the advantage

Here's the part nobody tells you: most agencies aren't doing this yet. They're still selling 2019 SEO packages. That means the AEO playing field is close to empty — and a small business that does the work now gets cited while competitors are still arguing about meta tags. You don't outspend anyone in AEO. You out-structure them.

Where to start

  1. Add complete schema markup — business type, service area, phone, pricing range.
  2. Build a real FAQ page that answers the questions customers actually ask, in full sentences.
  3. Make your Google Business Profile agree with your website exactly.
  4. Fix your site speed. If Google's crawler struggles, so does every AI.
