Why Your Wix Site Is Costing You Customers

← Insights  ·  Flint Digital  ·  3 min read

Nobody builds a Wix site because they love Wix. They build it because it was fast, cheap, and there. Fair enough — in 2019 that was a defensible call. It isn't anymore, and the reasons show up directly in your revenue.

The three ways a template site loses customers

1. Speed. Template builders ship megabytes of code your page doesn't use, because the builder has to support every feature for every customer. Your visitor pays for that in load time — and visitors on phones don't wait. Every extra second of load time costs you a measurable slice of them. A hand-coded page carries only what it needs and loads in under a second.

2. Search. Google and AI assistants read your site's code, not your design. Template output is a soup of nested wrappers and scripts with your actual content buried inside. Clean, semantic HTML with real schema markup tells search engines exactly what you do and where — which is why hand-coded sites rank with less effort.

3. Sameness. Your competitors used the same templates. When four local businesses look interchangeable, the customer picks on price. A site that looks and reads like nobody else's is how a small business avoids that race to the bottom.

“But rebuilding sounds expensive”

This is the myth that keeps people on templates. Add up what the template actually costs: the monthly builder fee, the apps and plugins, the ranking you never got, the visitors who bounced. We build hand-coded, SEO-structured sites for $1,500 flat — delivered in days, hosted fast, and you own the code outright. No platform holding your website hostage.

How to tell if your site has this problem

Run your site through Google's free PageSpeed test. Under 90 on mobile? You're losing people. Then search your own service in your own town — if you're not on page one and your site is a template, now you know why.
