FAQ

Straight answers.

No jargon, no fine print. If your question isn't here, call or email — we'll answer it the same way.

How much does a website cost?

A custom website build is $2,500 one-time. That includes design, development, mobile-first sub-second performance, an SEO and AEO foundation, analytics setup, and 30 days of post-launch support. Ongoing care plans start at $249/month and are optional.

Do I own my website?

Yes — completely. You own the code, the domain, the content, and all accounts. We don't lock you into a proprietary platform. If you ever leave, everything goes with you.

How long does it take to launch?

Days, not months. Our AI-accelerated build process ships most sites in a week or two depending on scope, without cutting corners on quality or performance.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO is making your business show up when customers ask AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Google's AI for a recommendation. It uses structured data, citations, reviews, and trustworthy content so the models recommend you instead of a competitor.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Care and growth plans are month-to-month — you can cancel anytime. We earn your business every month rather than locking you in.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in the Kansas City metropolitan area and serve small businesses across the region. Because our work is digital, we also work with clients elsewhere.

What platform do you build on?

We hand-code sites rather than using WordPress or page builders. That's how we hit sub-second load times and perfect performance scores, with no plugins to break or security holes to patch.

Do you manage Google Ads?

Yes. We run Expert-Mode Google Ads campaigns focused on conversions, not vanity clicks — included in the Dominate plan or available as a standalone service.

What if I already have a website?

We start with a free audit of what you have. Sometimes the right move is a rebuild; sometimes it's SEO, AEO, and ads on your existing site. We tell you honestly either way.

How do I get started?

Request a free audit from any page on this site, or book a call. We'll review your site, competitors, and AI visibility and show you where the opportunities are — usually within 60 seconds for the initial look.

Call, email, or just request your free audit — we'll get you answers.
